# Advancements

The mod adds several custom advancements to guide your progress and reward exploration.

## Phosphophyllite Tab
A dedicated advancement tab for the mod.

### I'M THE PHOSPHOPHYLLITE
**Root Advancement**
- **Description**: Obtained Phosphophyllite. Now you're officially shiny.
- **Trigger**: Obtain a Phosphophyllite item.
- **Frame**: Challenge (Purple)

### A Miner-al Mistake
- **Description**: Nutritious or toxic? Only one way to find out.
- **Trigger**: Eat any of the following items:
  - Raw Zinc
  - Zinc Ingot
  - Raw Corrupted Zinc
  - Phosphophyllite
  - Phosphophyllite Dust
- **Reward**: 10 Experience

### Are You Proud?
- **Description**: You ate a smithing template. Congratulations, I guess?
- **Trigger**: Eat a **Phosphophyllite Upgrade Smithing Template**.
- **Parent**: A Miner-al Mistake

### Ax-plosion
- **Description**: You tried to throw it... at bedrock... now everyone knows why that's a bad idea.
- **Trigger**: Throw a Phosphophyllite Axe at Bedrock.
