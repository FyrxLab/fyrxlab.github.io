# Enchantments

## Crystallization

**Crystallization** is a unique enchantment that can be applied to swords.

- **Max Level**: III
- **Target**: Swords
- **Effect**: Grants a chance to inflict the **Crystallization** status effect on the target when hit.
- **Duration**: 5 seconds (100 ticks).

### Effect Chances

The chance to trigger the effect increases with the enchantment level:

| Level | Chance |
|-------|--------|
| I     | 15%    |
| II    | 20%    |
| III   | 25%    |

### Interaction with Phosphophyllite Sword

The **Phosphophyllite Sword** has a natural 10% chance to inflict Crystallization. This enchantment **stacks** with that base chance:

| Level | Total Chance on Phos Sword |
|-------|----------------------------|
| None  | 10% (Base)                 |
| I     | 15%                        |
| II    | 20%                        |
| III   | 25%                        |

*(Note: The enchantment overrides the base chance with its higher value)*
