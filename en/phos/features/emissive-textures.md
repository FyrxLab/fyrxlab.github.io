# Emissive Textures

## Overview
Phosphophyllite ores support emissive textures when used with OptiFine or shader packs.

## Files
- **Base Textures**: Full ore textures with crystals
  - `0011.png` - Phosphophyllite Ore
  - `0111.png` - Deepslate Phosphophyllite Ore

- **Emissive Layers**: Only the glowing crystal portions
  - `phosphophyllite_ore_e.png` - Emissive layer for normal ore
  - `deepslate_phosphophyllite_ore_e.png` - Emissive layer for deepslate ore

## How to Use

### With OptiFine/Shaders
The emissive textures will automatically make only the crystal portions glow at full brightness, while the stone remains dark.

### Without OptiFine/Shaders
The ores emit light level 10 (redstone + 1), making the entire block glow slightly.
