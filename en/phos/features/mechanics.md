# Mechanics

## Ore Fortune Support
Phosphophyllite Ores are fragile but rich in material. Using the **Fortune** enchantment on your pickaxe significantly increases your yield.

- **No Fortune**: Drops 1 Phosphophyllite Dust.
- **Fortune I**: Drops 1-2 Phosphophyllite Dust.
- **Fortune II**: Drops 1-3 Phosphophyllite Dust.
- **Fortune III**: Drops 1-4 Phosphophyllite Dust.

## Crystallization Effect
A status effect that encases the target in crystal.
- **Source**: 
  - Phosphophyllite Sword (10% chance)
  - Phosphophyllite Axe (50% chance)
  - Crystallization Enchantment (15-25% chance)
- **Effect**: This encases the entity in crystal, severely limiting their movement and actions while dealing damage.

## Fragile Weaponry
Weapons made of Phosphophyllite are extremely sharp but brittle.
- **Breaking**: When a Phosphophyllite Sword or Axe breaks, it doesn't just disappear. It shatters into **Phosphophyllite Dust**.
- **Recovery**: This dust is automatically added to your inventory if possible, or dropped on the ground if your inventory is full. This allows you to recover some material to re-forge your weapons.
