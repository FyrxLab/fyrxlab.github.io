# Items & Blocks

## Ores

### Phosphophyllite Ore & Deepslate Phosphophyllite Ore
Found underground, these ores contain the precious Phosphophyllite Dust.
- **Drops**: 1 Phosphophyllite Dust (base).
- **Fortune Support**: 
  - Fortune I: 1-2 Dust
  - Fortune II: 1-3 Dust
  - Fortune III: 1-4 Dust
- **Harvest Level**: Iron Pickaxe or higher.

### Zinc Ore & Corrupted Zinc Ore
Found in both the Overworld and the Nether (Corrupted Zinc is the Nether variant). Smelt the raw drop in any furnace to get a Zinc Ingot.
- **Drops**: Raw Zinc / Raw Corrupted Zinc.
- **Smelting**: Raw Zinc → Zinc Ingot; Raw Corrupted Zinc → Zinc Ingot.
- **Harvest Level**: Iron Pickaxe or higher.

## Materials

### Phosphophyllite Dust
The primary material obtained from ores. Used for crafting and as a consumable — and as an improvised weapon.
- **Edible**: Yes. Can be eaten to obtain the "A Miner-al Mistake" advancement.
- **Melee use**: Hitting an entity with it in hand also applies the Crystallization effect for 5 seconds, the same as the Sword and Axe below.

### Phosphophyllite Gem
Refined form of the mineral.

### Zinc Ingot
Smelted from either Raw Zinc or Raw Corrupted Zinc. Used in crafting recipes for the mod's tools and upgrades.

## Weapons

### Phosphophyllite Sword
A fragile but sharp blade.
- **Attack Damage**: High
- **Durability**: Low
- **Special Effects**:
  - **Crystallization**: Has a natural 10% chance to inflict the Crystallization effect on hit.
  - **Fragile Nature**: When the sword breaks, it shatters into **1 Phosphophyllite Dust**, which is added to your inventory (or dropped if full).

### Phosphophyllite Axe
A versatile weapon that can be used in melee or thrown.
- **Attack Damage**: High
- **Durability**: Very Low (1 use)
- **Usage**:
  - **Melee**: Can be used to hit enemies directly.
  - **Ranged**: Can be thrown by holding right-click.
- **Special Effects**:
  - **Crystallization**: Has a **50% chance** to inflict the Crystallization effect on hit (both melee and thrown).
  - **Fragile Nature**: The axe breaks after a single use (hit or throw). When it breaks, it drops **1 Phosphophyllite Dust** into your inventory (or on the ground if full).

## Other Items

### Smithing Template (Phosphophyllite Upgrade)
Used to upgrade gear.
- **Edible**: Yes. Eating this grants the "**Are You Proud?**" advancement.
