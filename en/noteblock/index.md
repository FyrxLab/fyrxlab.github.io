## Installation

1. Download the latest version from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/noteblock) for your loader (Fabric or Forge).
2. Drop the `.jar` into your `mods/` folder — client and server both need it installed, since it adds real items and sounds.
3. Launch the game. The new discs are ready to find in-world or add via commands/creative inventory.

## Compatibility

| Loader | Minecraft Versions |
|--------|---------------------|
| Fabric | 1.12.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1 |
| Forge | 1.12.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1 |
