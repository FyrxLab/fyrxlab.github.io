# Absolute Energy System

Unlike a normal furnace that tracks burn time incrementally, the **Absolute Furnace** operates on a unified energy system called **Absolute Energy**.

## Generating Energy
Whenever you place a valid, burnable Minecraft item (such as Coal, Charcoal, or Wood) into any of the 6 Energy Input slots, the furnace instantly checks if there is space to store the resulting energy. 

If there is space, the fuel is consumed and converted into **Absolute Energy** items, which are stored directly in the energy slots.

## Consumption Rate
The smelting process is completely standardized:
* **Cost:** Exactly `2 Absolute Energy` per smelted item.
* **Speed:** Instantaneous per tick, constrained only by the availability of fuel and input materials.

> [!NOTE]
> Because it converts fuel into tangible Absolute Energy items, you will see Absolute Energy stacking up to 64 in the energy slots. This allows the furnace to buffer a massive amount of potential smelting power, ready to be unleashed the moment ores are inserted.
