# Getting Started

Welcome to **Absolute Furnace**! This advanced furnace is designed to alleviate the bottleneck of vanilla smelting by providing you with massive parallel processing power.

## Crafting & Placement
*(Wait for recipe implementation details or use JEI/REI)*
Once crafted, the Absolute Furnace can be placed like any normal block. It has a distinct front face and can be rotated horizontally.

## Understanding the GUI
When you right-click the Absolute Furnace, you'll open its custom GUI. You'll immediately notice it's significantly larger than a standard furnace:

- **Top Row (6 Slots):** Material Input. Place ores, raw meat, or anything you want to smelt here.
- **Side Slots (6 Slots):** Energy/Fuel Input. Place your coal, wood, or lava buckets here.
- **Bottom Row (6 Slots):** Material Output. Smelted items will appear here.

## How it works
The Absolute Furnace uses standard Minecraft smelting recipes. However, instead of consuming fuel directly per item, it converts any burnable fuel into a centralized power source called **Absolute Energy**. 

Simply place fuel in the energy slots, and place your items in the material input slots. The furnace will automatically distribute the work and smelt your items as fast as it can!
