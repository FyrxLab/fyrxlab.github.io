## Installation

1. Download the latest version from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/lazymod) for your loader (Fabric, Forge, or NeoForge).
2. Drop the `.jar` into your `mods/` folder.
3. Load into a world — your gear is waiting from the moment you spawn.

## Compatibility

| Loader | Minecraft Versions |
|--------|---------------------|
| Fabric | 1.12.2, 1.20.1, 1.21.1 |
| Forge | 1.12.2, 1.20.1, 1.21.1 |
| NeoForge | 1.20.1, 1.21.1 |

::: tip This one doesn't take itself too seriously
LazyMod is meant for casual/creative-style runs, not balanced survival — it hands you finished gear on purpose. If you want a fair fight, this isn't it.
:::
