## Installation

1. Download the latest version from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/lazymod) for your loader (Fabric, Forge, or NeoForge).
2. Drop the `.jar` into your `mods/` folder. This is the most physical effort this mod will ever ask of you.
3. Load into a world — your gear is waiting from the moment you spawn. You're welcome.

## FAQ

**Is this cheating?**
Yes. Obviously. You read the mod name and installed it anyway.

**Will this ruin my survival world?**
Only if "ruin" means "finish in ten minutes and have to find something else to do with your evening."

**Can I disable specific items, so it's only a little bit lazy?**
No. LazyMod does not support half-measures. Commit or don't install it.

**Is there a config file?**
There is not. Configuration is itself a form of effort.
